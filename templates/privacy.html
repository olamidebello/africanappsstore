Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Scope and contact

This policy describes Africa Apps Store at africanappsstore.space, operated by Olamide Olatayo Bello. For privacy questions or requests, email dobcalldemo@gmail.com. It covers the store; downloaded apps and linked websites have their own data practices.

Information stored

When you register, the store records your name, email address and a hashed password. It stores login sessions, account role, preferences, wishlist, download-library entries, reviews, publisher replies, beta or preregistration interests, reports and support tickets. Publisher records include app metadata and uploaded APK files. Administrative actions are recorded in an audit history.

Technical information and cookies

Requests necessarily transmit information such as an IP address, browser details and requested URL to the hosting infrastructure. Infrastructure logs may record request information for operation and security. The store uses an essential session cookie to keep you signed in; application sessions expire after about 24 hours. The current store application does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts. This does not describe independent apps or external websites.

How information is used

Information supports sign-in, app publication and downloads, account preferences, community features, support, abuse handling and service security. Download records populate your library and downloads also contribute to app counts. Reviews, publisher replies, app descriptions and developer display names can be visible to other visitors. Do not put private information in public submissions.

Access and sharing

Authorized administrators can access information needed to administer the store and handle support or reports. Hosting and infrastructure providers process information needed to deliver the service. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to investigate abuse and protect rights. Independent publishers and external services receive information when you interact with their apps or websites under their own policies.

Retention and security

Account and service records remain stored while needed for the service, support, security or legal obligations. Ask us to review deletion of your account and associated records. Some information may need to remain for lawful obligations or in backups until those backups expire. Password hashing, session controls and access checks help protect records, but no system guarantees absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can change available preferences, remove wishlist entries, leave beta or preregistration lists and edit or delete your own reviews. Email dobcalldemo@gmail.com to request access, correction, export or deletion of personal information, or to raise an objection where applicable. We may verify account ownership before acting. Rights and exceptions depend on applicable law; you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Children and updates

The store is not intended to collect children’s personal information without legally required authorization. If you believe a child supplied personal information improperly, contact us for review and removal as appropriate. We will update this page when practices change and provide further notice or seek consent when required.
